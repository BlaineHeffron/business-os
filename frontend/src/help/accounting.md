# Accounting

## What this does

Accounting shows sales, margin, unpaid invoices, and customers from your connected accounting system. It's a read-only view for checking business health and collections.

## How to operate it

- Read the cards for Sales this week, Sales this month, and Owed to you.
- Click a band in Unpaid invoices by age to filter the invoices below.
- Scan Open invoices for invoice, customer, due, balance, and status.
- Expand Customers to see the customer list.
- Refresh to pull the latest numbers right away.

## Common tasks

- Chase overdue money: click an age band in Unpaid invoices by age, then review the Open invoices it filters to.
- Track sales: compare Sales this week and Sales this month against the prior-period figures on each card.
- Look up a customer: expand Customers and scan company, email, tier, and active status.
