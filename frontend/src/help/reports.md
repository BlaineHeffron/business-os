# Reports

## What this does

Reports shows the owner digest — weekly and month-to-date summaries built from data already in BusinessOS. Use it to review performance before sending the digest to owners.

## How to operate it

- Review the weekly and month-to-date sections separately.
- Read the cards for sales, margin, calls, follow-ups, inventory, orders, and damage events.
- Read the written summary and callouts when they're available.
- Choose Generate now to build a fresh digest.
- Choose Email to owners to create a draft for the report recipients.

## Common tasks

- Prepare an owner update: choose Generate now, wait for the new digest, then review the numbers and summary.
- Share a digest: choose Email to owners, then open Gmail to review and send the draft.
- Confirm a number: read the card's footnote to see what it's based on.
- Review inventory health: compare stocked SKU count, out/critical counts, stocked valuation, and inbound value on open purchase orders. These use the same cached rules and totals as the Inventory report.
