# Leads

## What This Does

Leads is a review desk for potential sales opportunities from places the business has approved.

Findings are the actual opportunities awaiting a human decision. Sources are the approved places the team watches or manually checks.

## Common Tasks

- Review the Findings list first, then expand a row to inspect the evidence quote, source link, contact, company, and matched terms.
- Accept a good finding to send it to Queue for normal follow-up, or dismiss anything that is not relevant.
- Use Log a lead when a team member finds an opportunity in an enabled source manually.
- Open the Sources panel to see approved sources, watched markets, and buying-signal terms.

## What To Watch

Every finding needs a source and a concrete evidence quote. Manual capture stages the lead for review only; accepting it is still the step that creates Queue work.
